# Prompt templates

Replace `{{PRODUCT}}` with a short concrete description of the 3D printed item (e.g. “olive-sage green 3D printed minimalist temple gopuram decor”).  
Always attach the reference image. Keep “exact same product as the reference” in every prompt.

## 01 — Hero white

```text
Amazon-style main product photo of the exact same {{PRODUCT}} from the reference. Pure white RGB 255,255,255 background only. Straight-on front view, eye-level, product centered and filling about 85% of the frame. Soft even studio lighting, subtle soft shadow under the base. Photorealistic ecommerce product photography of a 3D printed product. No text, no props, no logos, no watermark.
```

## 02 — Three-quarter

```text
Product photo of the exact same {{PRODUCT}} from the reference, shown at a 45-degree three-quarter angle to reveal depth. Pure white background. Soft studio lighting, product fills most of the frame. Preserve materials and colors exactly. Clean ecommerce catalog style, no text.
```

## 03 — Side profile

```text
Product photo of the exact same {{PRODUCT}} from the reference in clear side profile so silhouette and depth are obvious. Pure white background, soft studio lighting, centered. Keep materials and accents faithful to the reference. No text, no props.
```

## 04 — Detail macro

```text
Macro close-up product detail shot of the exact same {{PRODUCT}} from the reference, focusing on texture, edges, and key accent details. Soft studio lighting, cream or white background softly out of focus. Show material finish and craftsmanship. No text overlays.
```

## 05 — Scale hand

```text
Scale reference product photo: a person's hand holding or gently presenting the exact same {{PRODUCT}} from the reference so buyers can judge real size. Clean light cream or white background, soft natural studio light. Product must match the reference design precisely. Ecommerce photography, no text.
```

## 06 — Lifestyle

```text
Lifestyle product photo: the exact same {{PRODUCT}} from the reference displayed in a tasteful real-home setting appropriate for the product. Soft natural light, calm premium atmosphere, product is the clear hero. Match reference colors and design exactly. Photorealistic, no text.
```

## 07 — Feature infographic

```text
Ecommerce feature callout image featuring the exact same {{PRODUCT}} from the reference on a soft cream background. Clean modern infographic with 3–5 short benefit callouts and thin leader lines. Elegant sans-serif text. Product large and central, matching the reference exactly. Marketplace-ready secondary listing image.
```

## 08 — Dimensions

```text
Product dimensions infographic of the exact same {{PRODUCT}} from the reference on white background. Front view with clean measurement arrows and labels for Height, Width, and Depth{{DIMENSIONS_CLAUSE}}. Soft studio look, readable typography. Product must match reference design. Ecommerce secondary image.
```

`{{DIMENSIONS_CLAUSE}}` examples:
- with known sizes: ` (use Height 22 cm, Width 12 cm, Depth 6 cm)`
- unknown: ` (use clearly marked placeholder dimensions and keep labels neat)`

## 09 — Packaging

```text
Packaging and unboxing flat-lay: the exact same {{PRODUCT}} from the reference beside an open premium kraft or gift box with soft tissue and a small care card. Warm cream surface, soft overhead studio lighting. Product clearly visible and matching the reference. Premium gift packaging aesthetic, no cluttered logos.
```

## 10 — Lifestyle trust

```text
Second lifestyle / trust-context photo of the exact same {{PRODUCT}} from the reference in an alternate real setting that builds purchase confidence (gift moment, entryway, shelf, or category-appropriate ritual/use). Soft warm ambient light. Product is the hero and matches the reference exactly. Photorealistic, no text overlays.
```

## 11 — Wall-placed (optional)

```text
Lifestyle product photo: the exact same {{PRODUCT}} from the reference mounted or placed on a clean light interior wall as wall decor. Soft natural daylight, subtle shadow showing wall placement. Modern calm home aesthetic. Product centered and matching the reference exactly. Photorealistic ecommerce lifestyle shot, no text.
```

## 12 — Desk-placed (optional)

```text
Lifestyle product photo: the exact same {{PRODUCT}} from the reference placed on a modern wooden desk as desk decor. Soft natural window light, shallow depth of field, minimal soft desk props in the background. Product is the hero and matches the reference exactly. Photorealistic ecommerce lifestyle shot, no text.
```
