# Shot list

Each image answers one buyer question. Keep product identity locked to the reference.

## Core (01–10) — always generate

| # | Filename suffix | Type | Buyer question | Production notes |
|---|-----------------|------|----------------|------------------|
| 01 | `hero-white` | Hero / main | What exactly am I buying? | Pure white `#FFFFFF`, ~85% fill, no text/props |
| 02 | `three-quarter` | Angle | How does it look in depth? | ~45° view, same lighting family as hero |
| 03 | `side-profile` | Profile | What’s the silhouette / thickness? | True side or clear profile |
| 04 | `detail-macro` | Detail | Does it feel cheap? | Close-up of texture, edges, accents, craftsmanship |
| 05 | `scale-hand` | Scale | How big is it? | Hand / body / familiar object for scale |
| 06 | `lifestyle` | Lifestyle | Will it fit my life? | Real room context; product is hero |
| 07 | `feature-infographic` | Infographic | What are the key benefits? | 3–5 short callouts; scannable |
| 08 | `dimensions` | Specs | What are the measurable details? | H × W × D; use real sizes if given |
| 09 | `packaging` | Unboxing | What arrives in the box? | Box, tissue, inclusions, gift feel |
| 10 | `lifestyle-trust` | Trust / second lifestyle | Why trust / gift this? | Alternate context (gift, shelf, ritual, brand) |

## Optional (11–12) — only if user asks

| # | Filename suffix | Type | Trigger examples | Production notes |
|---|-----------------|------|------------------|------------------|
| 11 | `wall-placed` | Wall lifestyle | “wall placed”, “wall mount”, “hanging on wall”, “wall decor” | Product on / against a clean wall; show wall placement clearly |
| 12 | `desk-placed` | Desk lifestyle | “desk placed”, “on the desk”, “desk decor”, “office desk” | Product on a desk/work surface; soft desk props OK |

## Gallery order tip (mobile)

When advising listing upload order, prefer:

1. Hero → 2. Infographic or lifestyle → 3. Scale/detail → remaining angles → packaging → optional wall/desk

## Category tweaks (3D prints)

- **Decor / sculptures / miniatures:** emphasize lifestyle, wall, desk, gift packaging
- **Functional prints (stands, organizers):** scale + in-use lifestyle matter most
- **Articulated / multi-part prints:** detail macro + what’s-in-box / assembly inclusions
- **Wearables / props:** scale on body/hand; skip wall/desk unless relevant
